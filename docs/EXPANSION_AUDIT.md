# Sidekin 100-Lineage Expansion Final Art Audit

Audit date: 2026-08-15

This audit covers the second 100 built-in lineages (catalog rows 101–200) and their 500 transparent five-stage assets. It supplements, rather than replaces, the original 100-lineage audit in docs/LINEAGE_AUDIT.md. Review began after every expansion lineup had been split, normalized to 1254×1254, and assembled into 20 asset contact sheets.

## Review Method

Every lineage was reviewed as a five-stage sequence for persistent identity, visible structural progression, readable separation between stages, species or existence-type continuity, complete uncropped forms, transparent output, and conspicuous generation artifacts. The audit used ArtSources/Expansion200/ReviewSheets/assets-01.jpg through assets-20.jpg; all 100 generated, processed, and reviewed checkpoints are recorded in ArtSources/Expansion200/progress.json.

- 100 expansion lineages and 500 described forms are integrated into the 200-lineage master catalog.

- The progress ledger records 100 generated lineups, 100 processed lineages, and 100 reviewed lineages.

This is internal reproducibility and visual-QA evidence. It is not external user validation, marketplace approval, production deployment, or an independent third-party art review.

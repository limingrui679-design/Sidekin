# Sidekin 2.1.0-beta.1

This prerelease doubles the built-in visual system while preserving Sidekin's local-first runtime and explicit evidence boundaries. The second corpus is integrated into the same application catalog, gallery, packaging gates, and release artifacts rather than remaining an authoring-only folder.

## Included

- 200 built-in lineages and 1,000 transparent `1254×1254` five-stage forms;
- the original balanced ten-category foundation plus 100 searchable tag-based expansion lineages;
- a completed 100/100 generation, processing, and manual-review ledger for the expansion;
- six visually repaired expansion lineages that were regenerated and reprocessed, plus one hash-pinned final asset normalization after the readability gate;
- separate legacy and expansion audit reports with 40 total final contact sheets;
- updated catalog generation, Swift provenance model, Electron gallery search, source verification, native packaging, and release gates;
- an all-200 inventory contact sheet generated from the application catalog and final assets.

## Trust boundary

The visual audits and automated checks are internal reproducibility evidence. They are not an independent art review, marketplace approval, user adoption, production deployment, or real-user impact evidence. The macOS CI archive is ad-hoc signed rather than Developer ID signed or notarized; the Windows archive is unsigned.

The repository and visual corpus remain proprietary under the included `LICENSE`. Public visibility does not grant reuse or redistribution rights.

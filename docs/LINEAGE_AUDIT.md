# Sidekin 100-Lineage Final Art Audit

Audit date: 2026-08-13

This is the final post-production review of the complete 500-image set. Review began only after all 100 lineages and all five canonical stages existed. Each row below represents a manual inspection of the five forms together at desktop scale, supported by the 20 final contact sheets in `ArtSources/AuditSheets/`.

## Review Method

Every lineage was checked for:

- a plausible progression from origin to hatchling, juvenile, ascended, and legendary form;
- persistent face, core, facade, palette, material, or other identity anchors;
- no unintended species, object class, or existence-type switch;
- enough structural difference between adjacent stages to avoid recolor-only or accessory-only growth;
- anatomy or construction appropriate to the declared subject;
- no real person, human child, realistic human skin, realistic human hair, or ordinary human face in a humanoid design;
- a complete transparent cutout with no cropped component or key-color damage;
- a legible silhouette and focal feature at the floating pet's 205–235 px scale.

The audit and README showcase follow-up produced 83 raw repair candidates for 80 unique stage targets across 63 lineages. Some targets needed more than one candidate before acceptance. Accepted resources were normalized to the same `1254×1254` transparent format and then rechecked in their regenerated final sheet. A lineage with accepted replacement evidence is marked with a repair result; all others passed the complete-set review without a new replacement.

The rows are now organized only by ordinal range. Sidekin's live catalog uses overlapping free-form tags and does not assign these lineages to fixed categories.

## Final Automated Gate Results

The final corpus passed the following checks after the manual review and repairs:

- 100 catalog themes and 500 deterministic stage prompts;
- exactly 500 binary-unique final PNG files;
- exact `1254×1254` dimensions and transparent corners for every asset;
- visible alpha occupancy between 4.5% and 72%;
- high-silhouette candidates below the paired appearance rejection thresholds;
- 30 local self-tests, 6 mocked API tests, Debug build, and Release build;
- no CJK text in the English product surface, catalog, prompts, scripts, or project documentation.

No real API key was read and no paid API call was made by the application test suite. The application was not launched during this dated art-only audit; current runtime launch evidence is recorded separately in `docs/COMPLETION_AUDIT.md`.
